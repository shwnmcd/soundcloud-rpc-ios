SoundCloud S
============

An ad-free SoundCloud desktop client with Discord Rich Presence,
worldwide Group Jam and multiplayer mini-games.

CREDITS
-------
SoundCloud S is based on SoundCloud RPC by richardhbtz
https://github.com/richardhbtz/soundcloud-rpc
SoundCloud RPC is released under the MIT License.

SoundCloud S is an unofficial client and is not affiliated with,
endorsed by, or sponsored by SoundCloud.

INSTALLING & REMOVING
---------------------
- On the next screens you can choose who to install for and the
  folder where SoundCloud S will be installed.
- To remove SoundCloud S later, use any of:
    * Settings > About > Uninstall SoundCloud S (inside the app)
    * Windows Settings > Apps > Installed apps > SoundCloud S
    * Start Menu > SoundCloud S > Uninstall SoundCloud S
  Uninstalling also removes your local SoundCloud S settings.
